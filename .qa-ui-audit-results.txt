Root cause: `Number(x).toLocaleString(..., { minimumFractionDigits: 2 })`
without `maximumFractionDigits: 2` lets float math (e.g. tax %, discount %)
render 3+ decimals (`$1,234.567`). LeadDetail Profit also passed the negative
sign through `toLocaleString` so it landed AFTER the `$` prefix giving `$-450.25`
instead of the standard `-$450.25`.

Fix: New shared util at client/src/utils/currency.js exporting
`formatCurrency(value)` — handles negative sign placement, NaN/null fallback
to `$0.00`, and enforces both min and max fraction digits to 2. Migrated 20+
inline call sites and consolidated 2 local helpers onto the shared util.

Helpers left intact (already-correct or different input shape):
- utils/financing.js#formatMoney        — takes CENTS, not dollars
- AdminDashboard.jsx#formatMoney        — takes CENTS, not dollars
- Dashboard.jsx#formatCurrency          — intentional K/M abbreviation
- Pipeline.jsx#formatCurrency           — intentional K/M abbreviation, returns null for 0
- StormProperties.jsx#formatCurrency    — intentional no-decimals for assessed values
